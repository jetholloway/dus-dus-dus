How to play
  • Click one of your pieces to see what it can do.
  • Move: click a highlighted empty square.
  • Pass: with the ball carrier selected, click a highlighted team-mate.
  • Tackle: select a piece next to the ball, click the ball, then click where to go.
  • Click the selected piece again, or press Escape, to cancel.

The full rules are in the rulebook in the project's docs folder.
